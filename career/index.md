## 👔 Career

### Interview Prep

- ⭐ [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/) - The de facto standard for coding interview practice. People recommends doing the bulk of your practice here.
- ⭐ [NeetCode.io](https://neetcode.io/) - Practice the Blind 75 and NeetCode 150 with a clean checklist and free YouTube explanations for every problem. Constantly recommended.
- [Tech Interview Handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/tech-interview-handbook) - Covers algorithms, behavioral questions, salary negotiation, and resume tips.
- [Grind 75](https://www.techinterviewhandbook.org/grind75/) - Created by the author of Blind 75; a customizable study plan that tells you exactly which problems to do and in what order.
- [Big-O Cheat Sheet](https://www.bigocheatsheet.com/) - Quick reference for time and space complexity of common algorithms and data structures.
- [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) - The most recommended free system design resource on Reddit. Covers scalability, databases, caching, and more with Anki flashcards.
- [Back To Back SWE (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/c/BackToBackSWE) - Free YouTube channel with clear visual explanations of LeetCode problems; 
- [NeetCode (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/c/NeetCode) - Free YouTube explanations for every NeetCode 150 problem. 
- [Interviewing.io](https://interviewing.io/) - Practice real anonymous mock interviews with engineers from top companies. Free to use as an interviewee.
- [Front End Interview Handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/front-end-interview-handbook) - Like Tech Interview Handbook but focused on HTML, CSS, JS, and browser internals.
- [HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/) - Good for domain-specific practice (SQL, regex, data structures) and certification badges recognized by recruiters.
- [GeeksForGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/) - Needs no introduction. You will find the article you are looking for, here.
- [CSES Problem Set](https://cses.fi/problemset/) - ~300 well-curated algorithmic problems. Beloved by the competitive programming community as structured free practice.

### Résumé & Portfolio

- [JSON Resume](https://jsonresume.org/) - Open standard for resumes using a portable JSON schema; dozens of free themes available.
- [Resume Builder by blopa](https://github.com/blopa/Resume-Builder) - Free open-source resume builder using Google Spreadsheets or JSON; no login required.
- [GitHub Topics: developer-portfolio](https://github.com/topics/developer-portfolio) - Index of open-source portfolio site templates you can fork and deploy for free.

Note: Here you can use stuff like Typst to make your resume yourself instead of Word or LaTeX. 

### Salary & Jobs

- [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/) - Crowdsourced salary database for tech; more accurate than Glassdoor for SWE compensation data.
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/) - Free job board for startups; filter by role, salary, equity, and remote work.
- [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/) - Anonymous community where verified tech workers discuss salaries, interviews, and company culture.
