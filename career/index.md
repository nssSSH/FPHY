# Career

## Interview prep

- ⭐ [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/) - The largest coding practice platform with thousands of questions; Very algorithmic heavy and not project based. 

- [Tech Interview Handbook](https://github.com/yangshun/tech-interview-handbook) - Curated coding interview prep by the author of Blind 75; covers algorithms, behavioral questions, and negotiation. 

- [Coding Interview University](https://github.com/jwasham/coding-interview-university) - Multi-month study plan for becoming a software engineer for a large company.

- [System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) - Learn how to design large-scale systems. Prep for the system design interview. Includes Anki flashcards. 

- [GeeksForGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/) - Needs no introduction. You will find the article you are looking for, here. 

- [HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/) - Coding practice platform.
