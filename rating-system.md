Before recomending a resource/book/site, it is important to rate them.

Since it is impossible to go through every single book, a lot of these rating are crowd sourced from reddit/similar sites.

Hence, the guides (which I shall be using to refer to books/any other resources) will be judged across 5 different areas:

- Content Coverage
- Accuracy
- Practicality
- Career relavance
- Difficulty

Within each areas, there are multiple questions to be asked.

Such as (but not limited to):

- Does it cover exercises/projects?
- Are explanations technically correct?
- Are examples modern?
- How much real coding is involved?
- Is it open source?
- Is it updated frequently?
- Does it prepare for career?

Obviously, this criteria cannot be applied uniformly across all guides. For example, books are not updated frequently, but it is unfair to deduct points from them just for this reason alone. They serve a different purpose altogether. 

The total points are given out of 10. 2 points for each criteria.
